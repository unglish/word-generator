# Q13c unit-normalization protocol preparation

This directory freezes the design, endpoints, control identities, fixtures,
certificate contract and timing plan before generator behavior changes. It is
outside the frozen top-level quality evaluator's source digest.

`protocol.json` is the machine-readable registration; its byte SHA-256 is
recorded in `registration.json`. `design.md` is the approved read-only design.
The control is exact published #331. Its complete public-API parity study allows
the immutable #328 archive to remain the behavioral control. No archive is
regenerated by this preparation.

`observe.ts` is a pure **historical v1/v2 structural observer**. It replays exact
cells and counts current dedup events, final source survival and unavailable
prospective evidence. It does not verify English readings, probabilities or new
normalization certificates. Unknown versions/capabilities fail. The candidate
adapter and production license verifier remain unimplemented; they must be
reviewed and frozen before any behavior capture. No unknown or absent episode is
counted as a successful normalization.

Report emitted and verified certificate counts alongside the replay fraction.
If no certificates were emitted, the fraction is `null` with status
`not-applicable`, never numeric 100%. Historical missing evidence is
`unavailable`. The required successful controlled fixture rules out a retain-all
implementation; there is no minimum sampled emission target.

`certificate-contract.ts` specifies a separate local emitted-normalization
family. An original sampled selection remains the historical state for later
sampling and quota decisions. The certificate must rederive its pre-unit state
from actual original choices and the detached writer boundary, verify positive
alternative support and allowed doubling, and check every changed neighbor's
reading dependency at the existing operation point. Its probabilities describe
local eligibility support, never a counterfactual whole-word probability.
The cursor locates even a retained/no-edit observation in append/edit history.
The future v3 verifier must honor `lastAppendedUnitId` for mid-writer snapshots;
the historical append-all-units-first replay is only valid for its existing
after-assembly coverage certificates, not for new local certificate snapshots.

The candidate must use a new ledger version/capability and a distinct normalized
cell origin. Both the whole unit and its exact ordered phone IDs are preserved.
No construction may acquire joint ownership by copying rewrite ancestry or
merging two equal-sound phone IDs. Original v1/v2 records remain readable.

The first slice proposes only the legacy first-character shortening and admits
context-complete single-phone targets. Unsupported marker/shared/open-vowel
obligations remain refusals. Sampling history does not change retrospectively.
The cap planner must freeze normalized live spans; if a whole-word certificate
would use stale realization state, record `normalization-context-unavailable`.
Numeric caps, the 8,192-state budget and existing gates remain unchanged.

Run the preparation checks with:

```sh
node --import tsx --test evaluation/quality/probes/unit-normalization/observe.test.ts
```

The 64 fixture records are exact archived extractions, with all 31 partial-th
witnesses and other mechanism examples. The gzipped bytes and source identity
are pinned. One additional authenticated record supplies an existing coverage
certificate for structural identity/offset corruption tests. Its reading and
probability verification remains outside this observer. A synthetic v1 schema
conversion is labeled as such in its test.
Pure observer fixtures are not public-generator behavior tests; the registered
runtime fixture matrix and API parity studies remain mandatory before capture.

The historical audit's baseline counts are descriptive. Matching a remainder in
the inventory (112 partial cuts) is not proof of operative eligibility: only 60
even have single-phone target metadata, before conditions, weights, doubling and
neighbor checks. No target success count is fitted to these observations.

Do not update the registration after seeing candidate results. Required protocol
amendments must be reviewed and recorded before behavior/capture, preserving
earlier bytes and explaining the reason. Failures against unchanged gates stay
visible; a mechanical coverage gain is not a reader-preference result.
