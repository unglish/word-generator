# Completion-only isolated /u/ support

The sole intervention enables completion-only `oo` for isolated /u/ with weight 3. All 151 runtime source files are byte-identical to control PR #372; initial writing configuration is unchanged.

Full registered capture and replay cover 200,000 words across 20 streams. Independent recount performed 3,800,019 integer comparisons. Final unresolved obligations decreased from 22 to 2; satisfied obligations increased from 23,255 to 23,390. Continuous RNG divergence means this is a population comparison, not a claim that exactly twenty baseline words were repaired. The remaining trace witnesses are `julicky` and `sulkicmarened`, with neighbor-reading refusals. Zero-unresolved acceptance is unmet.

Legacy parity passed 83,072 generation calls and 128 next-value probes. The first parity dispatch failed before generation because its control materialization manifest was absent; authenticated restoration and the failed log are retained.

Default suite: 1,024 passed, 10 failed, 4 skipped. Four named failures match the control; five test timeouts, one dependent n-gram assertion and a phonotactic hook timeout required follow-up. All five affected files passed sequentially with original timeouts and thresholds. This does not turn the original full-suite failure into a pass. Quality suite: 10 passed, 2 failed (five-consonant count 83 and owngs count 62, same as control). Full lint retains seven errors identical to control. Trace audit exit 0 is command completion, not proof all quality targets pass.

Six original performance pairs completed: median throughput change -0.6054%; paired range -10.3659% to +15.6881%. Both arms passed variance 6/6 and failed speed 6/6. No speed improvement is established.

Distribution side effects are mixed: unique spellings change by -74 default, -12 bare, +45 monosyllables and -26 text. Trigram Jensen-Shannon divergence decreases in default, monosyllables and text, but increases in bare; default and bare missing reference trigram mass increase. These descriptive comparisons do not establish general quality improvement.

The evidence index authenticates bytes only. Full structure/distribution reports, observations, operators, individual timing records, failed logs and remaining witnesses are included. Raw capture stays outside Git. Human output-quality outcomes remain unmeasured. This candidate has not yet completed review or publication.
