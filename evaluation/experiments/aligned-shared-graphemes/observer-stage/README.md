This stage adds measurement tools after 08c1a57. It changes no generator source,
English default, frozen baseline, registered law, metric threshold or random
stream. No formal candidate corpus or distribution improvement is claimed.

The shared observer distinguishes unavailable v3 eligibility from available v4
counts. Per-rule scans, original matching windows, ownership/policy/neighbor
refusals, eligible trials, failures, formations and RNG draw/skip counts are
retained. Structural counting reconstructs edit prefixes, requires complete
original or certified repaired input extents, compares ordered phones and sound
sequences, and follows shared cells across later edits. It detects prior partial
source loss, explicit source mismatch, repeated phone use, erasure and insertion
inside a surviving multi-letter construction. Explicit lexical supersession is
separate. These are arithmetic/structural checks, not independent reading-license
semantics; the production verifier runs separately.

New repair and doubling readers preserve the preceding metrics while accepting
v4's multi-unit origins and immutable original sampler records. Historical
readers remain untouched. Tests compare every repair counter and the complete
doubling observation on 500 public legacy words. Another 500 public active words
are replayed and observed, with no structural violation in that fixture sample.
That sample requires some formation, not measured positive use of every rule;
all-three-rule corpus use remains a required endpoint.

The aggregator retains overlapping profile, stream, syllable, morphology,
resolved-affix, written-length and root-phone-length groups, selected/shared-form
histograms and complete first witnesses. Historical shared counts and per-rule
counts are null, not clean zeros. Mixed availability is explicit. Original
selection forms never imply a final assembled pronunciation.

identity, complete streams and draw ordering, and leaves a failure artifact
